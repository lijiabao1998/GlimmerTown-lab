# Wave 14 C1 supplemental visual review

Reviewed the 57 existing CI PNG files listed below directly with view_image on 2026-10-24. No images were edited, rerendered, cropped, or reconstructed; no source files were changed. This is a bounded visual QA finding, not owner approval.

## Findings

- Five near-scene targets (mereweigh, rushfordlav, woldswitch, larkprint, and rowdenmortuary, using filename identifiers): all target buildings are visible. At captured scale, no obvious target clipping, detached wall faces, missing roof planes, or open roof/wall gaps were observed. Some neighboring scenery is outside the near-view frame, which is camera framing rather than target clipping.
- Twenty edge screenshots: all four rotations for all five assigned buildings retain intact silhouettes on the corner platform. No apparent clipping into the map-edge cutaway or water, detached foundations, or conspicuous roof/wall discontinuities observed. Mereweigh's tower/low-wing connection, Rushfordlav's cross-shaped roofs, Woldswitch's tower/lower wings, Larkprint's tall block/workshop, and Rowdenmortuary's two volumes remain coherent in the inspected angles.
- Four winter-snow day rotations: building silhouettes and location are retained; snow/white edge highlights strongly soften small roof and facade details. These are not sufficient to certify every fine join.
- Four summer-night rotations and rain-night: buildings remain legible beneath the ambient blue tint, with warm window light where visible. Pale blue vertical shafts extending to the frame top are conspicuous. Parent reports these are preexisting landmark/selection overlays confirmed in prior waves; they are recorded as preserved context, not a new-art failure. No global lighting change is proposed.
- Construction early/middle/late: the sequence shows foundations, partial scaffolding/cranes, then multiple recognizable buildings within scaffold frames. Stage completion is staggered across lots; some later lots remain foundation-only in the shown frames. No obvious floating scaffold component or target asset clipping was identified at the captured scale. Static captures cannot establish transition timing or animation correctness.
- Ten day preview PNGs (r1/r2 of each target): isolated sprites show coherent visible joins and complete visible bases at native resolution. Ten night preview PNGs (r0/r2 of each target) display sparse emissive details on transparent/black presentation, not full lit buildings; they were not treated as composited night-scene evidence.

## Scope and limitations

No new obvious visual blocker found in the inspected subset. Review is limited to these static PNGs and their displayed/native scale. No live run, animation, interaction, hover, pixel-diff, source-intent audit, or all-file/all-season inspection was performed. Other rotations of isolated previews, other seasonal scene sets, legacy comparison scenes, and the parent's previously reviewed images are outside this review. Snow and night overlays obscure fine detail. Absence of an obvious issue here is not a claim of universal defect freedom.
